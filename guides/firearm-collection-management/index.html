Firearm collection management

Build a useful record for every firearm, from your first inventory to a portable collection catalog.

Start with a consistent inventory

Give each firearm its own record. Capture the maker, model, serial number, configuration, and purchase details you know. Keep uncertain information clearly marked rather than filling gaps with guesses. A consistent naming system makes it easier to find a record later.

Bring existing records together

If you already maintain a spreadsheet, review it for duplicate entries and inconsistent names before importing from Excel or CSV. Add photographs and supporting documents to the matching firearm record. Voice-to-text can help capture notes without typing every field.

Keep the record current

Add maintenance work and range sessions as they happen, with a date and enough detail to understand the event later. Armur keeps these records associated with the specific gun, so you can review its history without searching through separate folders.

Keep a portable copy

Export a professional PDF catalog when you need an organized view of your collection. Include photographs, provenance documents, maintenance records, and range sessions. Choose local-only storage or encrypted sync according to how you want to access your records.
