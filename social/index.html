What you can do

Make a connection around a shared interest.

Ask, discuss, and learn

Bring a focused question to other enthusiasts. Add the background you know, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and use the conversation to find sources worth following up.

Invite your circle

Invite other shooters and collectors to Armur. Bring together the people you already share a club, collection interest, or sporting tradition with.

Refer trusted specialists

Refer gun appraisers and gunsmiths to fellow members. Describe the kind of work or appraisal you have experience with so others can understand the context of your recommendation.

Social

The conversation with others

Exchange perspectives with the community. A discussion can offer new leads and connections while your vault remains the home for your collection records.

Participate thoughtfully.

The Social feed is for authenticated app members. This public website explains the feature; it does not display members’ discussions or private collection records.

Share the information relevant to a conversation. Review photographs and documents before posting, especially when they contain serial numbers, addresses, or someone else’s personal information.

A useful contribution has context

  • Explain what you know and what you are trying to learn.
  • Credit the sources behind a historical claim.
  • Describe your own experience when making a referral.
  • Keep discussion respectful of other collectors and their choices.
